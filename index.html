Personal automation tool · Herramienta personal

n8n Juan

A private assistant that reads my Gmail once a day and sends only me a short summary of what needs attention.

What it does

  • Every morning at 7:00 a.m. (Central Time) it reads the sender, subject, labels and a short preview of the emails received in the last 24 hours.
  • It uses an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) to write a summary sorted by priority: urgent, needs a reply, for your information, and promotions.
  • It emails that summary to the account owner. Nothing else.

What it never does

  • It never replies to, deletes, archives, forwards or sends email to anyone else.
  • It never sells, shares or publishes email data.
  • It is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner.

En español

n8n Juan es una herramienta personal de automatización. Cada mañana lee el remitente, el asunto, las etiquetas y un pedacito del texto de los correos de las últimas 24 horas, y le envía solo a su dueño un resumen ordenado por importancia. Nunca responde, borra, reenvía ni envía correos a otras personas, y no vende ni comparte ningún dato.
